Anuradha Wickramarachchi, PhD

Adelaide, Australia
•
anuradha.c.wickramarachchi@gmail.com

I am a Software Engineer at AML3D developing algorithms for advanced manufacturing applications (Wire Arc Additive Manufacturing – WAAM). With a PhD in Computer Science from the Australian National University (ANU) and background as a Solutions Architect at CSIRO, I bridge software engineering, computational science, and research across advanced manufacturing, distributed cloud systems, and machine learning.

Previously at Commonwealth Scientific and Industrial Research Organisation (CSIRO), I designed and built solutions for population-scale health and biosecurity problems that scale to real-world population sizes.

I am passionate about high-performance computing, distributed cloud systems, data engineering, bioinformatics, and computational geometry.

Software Engineer

Solutions Architect

Commonwealth Scientific and Industrial Research Organisation (CSIRO)

2022 - 2024 • Canberra, Australia

Designed and built solutions for population-scale health and biosecurity problems with the Transformational Bioinformatics Group.

  • Architected and built cloud-native distributed platforms and pipelines handling population-scale genomics and infectious disease biosecurity data.
  • Designed scalable genomic search and data exchange protocols including GA4GH Beacon standards (sBeacon, AskBeacon).
  • Led DevOps, Infrastructure as Code (Terraform), and cloud infrastructure architecture across distributed teams.
  • Awarded CSIRO Entrepreneurship Award 2024 (Indonesia biosecurity platform) and CSIRO Science & Engineering Award 2022 (SARS-CoV-2 monitoring capability).
